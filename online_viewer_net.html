elbMUN 2025

Strategic Communications, Social Media & Institutional PR

A year-long communications strategy for a student-led Model United Nations conference, designed to increase national visibility, strengthen institutional credibility, and attract participants beyond the existing MUN community.

Social Media StrategyInstitutional PRContent PlanningCommunity TrustCampaign Storytelling

What I delivered

  • Social media strategy and campaign planning
  • Speaker and institutional communication assets
  • Testimonial content and delegate-facing storytelling
  • Cross-channel PR coordination across Instagram, email, website, and partner communication
  • Visual content for awareness, credibility, and event engagement

Case study overview

As Head of PR & Media for elbMUN 2025, I developed and executed a communications strategy that positioned the conference as a credible, nationally visible student-led event. My work combined social media storytelling, institutional outreach, speaker communication, and trust-building content to expand awareness beyond the existing MUN network and reinforce the conference’s public relevance.

The challenge

elbMUN 2025 needed to attract students across Germany, compete with other MUN conferences, and build enough credibility to secure high-level institutional speakers and partnerships.

  • Low awareness beyond the existing MUN community
  • Need for stronger institutional legitimacy
  • National audience from a Dresden-based conference
  • Strong competition within the MUN space

My strategy

The strategy focused on three parallel tracks: digital community building, institutional outreach, and earned credibility through partnerships.

  • Visibility — Instagram-led public-facing promotion
  • Credibility — speaker and institutional positioning
  • Trust — testimonial content and social proof

Results

The campaign helped position elbMUN 2025 as a nationally visible student conference with stronger institutional recognition and audience reach.

  • 133 participants across 4 committees
  • Led a PR & Media team of 5
  • 2 high-profile institutional speakers secured
  • Up to 7,805 accounts reached per post

Selected campaign assets

Asset 01 — Conference Launch

Launch post introducing elbMUN 2025 through a clear visual identity, event information, and a public-facing conference image. This asset established the campaign’s tone and made the event immediately legible to new audiences.

Asset 02 — Institutional Speaker Announcement

Carousel post announcing Alexander Dierks, President of the Saxon State Parliament, as a closing speaker. This content was designed to strengthen institutional credibility and signal that elbMUN had relevance beyond the student sphere.

Asset 03 — Delegate Testimonial

Testimonial video created to build trust through authentic delegate experience. This format functioned as social proof, helping prospective participants imagine themselves at the conference and reducing uncertainty around applying.

Asset 04 — Event Recap

Short-form recap video capturing the atmosphere and momentum of the conference. This content extended the campaign beyond pre-event promotion and became one of the strongest-performing outputs in the communication cycle.

Flip the Switch.
Enpal Campaign

Speculative Campaign Strategy / University Marketing Seminar

A research-based multi-channel campaign concept designed to make Enpal feel more human, visible, and emotionally relatable through storytelling, influencer strategy, outdoor advertising, and experiential activations.

Campaign StrategyBrand PositioningSocial StorytellingOOH ConceptExperiential Marketing

What I developed

  • Campaign concept and slogan development
  • Audience and communication problem analysis
  • Strategic framework across social, influencer, outdoor, and experiential channels
  • Documentary-style social content concept
  • Campaign controls, timeline, and implementation logic

Case study overview

Developed for a university marketing seminar, this project is a speculative multi-channel campaign concept for Enpal. “Flip the Switch” was designed to humanize the brand, strengthen visibility, and connect solar energy to everyday life through storytelling, outdoor advertising, influencer partnerships, and experiential activations.

The challenge

The campaign began with a clear diagnosis: Enpal lacked an emotionally resonant and relatable brand presence across key audience groups, especially beyond already engaged consumers.

  • Solar still feels technical or distant to non-customers
  • Interested audiences are not reached effectively offline
  • Social communication feels insufficiently human
  • Existing customers lack belonging and advocacy logic

My strategy

The campaign strategy combined three complementary tracks designed to make Enpal feel more relatable, memorable, and desirable.

  • Relatable storytelling — everyday-life solar narratives
  • Trusted visibility — influencer and OOH amplification
  • Experiential engagement — pop-up and community activations

Deliverables

This concept campaign translated diagnosis into a coherent cross-channel execution system, from hero message to activation format.

  • Concept and slogan: “Flip the Switch”
  • Campaign architecture and audience logic
  • Social, influencer, OOH, and event planning
  • Visual mockups and implementation direction

Selected campaign assets

Asset 01 — Campaign Identity

The campaign identity centered on the slogan “Flip the Switch”, designed to frame solar adoption as simple, empowering, and emotionally accessible rather than technical or intimidating.

Asset 02 — Problem Definition

This slide maps the communication problem across three audience groups — non-customers, interested customers, and existing customers — showing how the campaign was built from audience-specific barriers rather than generic promotion.

Asset 03 — Campaign Framework

The campaign was structured around three interconnected pillars: emotional storytelling, trusted visibility, and experiential community activation. This gave the concept a coherent logic across all channels.

Asset 04 — Social Storytelling

These documentary-style reel concepts position solar energy as part of real, lived experience. Instead of purely technical messaging, the campaign links Enpal to family routines, mobility, and everyday aspiration.

Asset 05 — Influencer Strategy

The influencer strategy paired campaign messaging with creator-led credibility, using relatable communication and cross-platform adaptation to extend reach beyond brand-owned channels.

Asset 06 — Outdoor Advertising

Outdoor placements across Saxony were designed to increase physical visibility and reinforce brand recall through bold, curiosity-driven messaging in high-traffic public spaces.

Asset 07 — Pop-Up Events

Pop-up stands and indoor events extended the campaign from awareness into direct engagement, using consultation, demos, and live interaction to humanize the brand and create trust.
